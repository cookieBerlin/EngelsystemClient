--- title: Engelsystem Client ---

Your Engelsystem shifts, on iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch

An unofficial companion app for Engelsystem, the volunteer-management system used by events like the Chaos Communication Congress. See your shifts, news and messages, and get reminded before a shift starts.

Join the beta on TestFlight Read the user guide

Beta · iOS / iPadOS / watchOS 26.5 or later · open source (GPL-2.0)

Features

  • Your shifts, grouped by day, with the location one tap away
  • News and announcements, including comments
  • Your profile, angel types and work logs
  • Rooms and locations of the event
  • Private messages (on servers that support them)
  • Local reminders before each shift — no server push
  • Several events at once
  • Apple Watch app and complication for your next shift
  • Demo Mode to try everything without an account

Your data stays yours

The app talks directly to your event's Engelsystem server with your own API key. There is no account with the developer, no analytics and no tracking. Read the privacy policy.
